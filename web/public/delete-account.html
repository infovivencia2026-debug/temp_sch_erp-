Delete your account

EDU CLOUD school app · Developer: [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME] · App: com.schoolerp.parent

Your EDU CLOUD account is created for you by your school, and your child's records belong to the school. You can ask for your account and the personal data linked to it to be deleted at any time. Here is how, and what happens.

How to request deletion

  1. In the app: open Account (your name at the top or in the menu) → Delete my account. This sends the request to your school office and to us.
  2. By email: write to [SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the email address or mobile number on your account, with the subject "Delete my account", your name and your school's name. If you are a parent, name the child(ren) linked to your account.
  3. At the school: ask the school office. The school can remove your sign-in and unlink you from a child immediately.

We will confirm receipt within 72 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days. We may need to verify that the request comes from you.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: email/mobile, password hash, sessions, app-lock and notification settings, and the push-notification token for your phone.
  • Your profile photo and any documents you uploaded yourself.
  • Messages and forum posts you wrote, where the school does not need to retain them.
  • The link between your account and any child's record, so you no longer see that child.
  • The offline copy of pages kept on your phone (deleted the moment you sign out or uninstall).

What the school must keep

Some records are the school's records, not your account, and Indian education, tax and accounting law requires the school to retain them for a set period even after you leave:

  • The student's admission record, attendance registers, marks and issued certificates (transfer certificate, report cards).
  • Fee invoices, receipts and payment history (typically 8 years under tax rules), including prepaid-wallet transactions.
  • Records needed to resolve a dispute or comply with a legal order.

These are kept by the school under its own retention schedule, are no longer linked to a working login, and are not used for any other purpose. When a student leaves, their record is moved to the school's leavers/alumni register rather than erased, so a certificate can still be issued later.

Deleting the app only

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone but does not delete your account, you can sign in again on the website. To delete the account, use one of the routes above.
