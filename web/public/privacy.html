1. Who is responsible for your data

Your school decides what information is recorded about you or your child and who at the school may see it. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) the school is the Data Fiduciary for that information.

[OPERATOR LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us") builds and operates the EDU CLOUD platform and processes the data on the school's instructions, as its Data Processor. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Why the data is used

  • To run the school: mark and show attendance, publish homework and results, raise and collect fees, plan transport, and communicate with families.
  • To sign you in securely and keep your session on your device.
  • To send you notifications you can act on, a circular, the bus near your stop, a fee due, an absence.
  • To keep records the school is required by law to keep (for example fee receipts and attendance registers).
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse and fix faults.

4. Children's data

The Service holds information about students, most of whom are children. This information is recorded by the school in the course of educating the child, and the school obtains any consent the law requires from the parent or lawful guardian at admission. The app is for parents, guardians, staff and older students; it does not knowingly let a child create an account, and it does not use children's data for tracking, profiling or advertising. A parent's account shows only the children linked to it by the school.

6. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). The app refuses unencrypted connections.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes; we cannot read them.
  • Each school's data is isolated at the database level (row-level security), so one school cannot see another's records.
  • Access inside a school is limited by role and, where applicable, to the classes or children a person is responsible for.
  • The app can be locked with your phone's fingerprint or face unlock. It keeps a copy of recently viewed pages on your phone so it works offline; that copy is deleted when you sign out.

7. How long data is kept

Your school decides retention in line with education and tax law. As a guide: attendance, marks and fee records are kept for the period the law requires (fee receipts typically 8 years under Indian tax rules); a student who leaves is moved to the school's alumni register rather than erased, so that certificates can still be issued; sign-in sessions expire automatically; server logs are kept for a short period for security. When you or the school asks for deletion (see §9), data that the school is not legally required to keep is deleted within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask to access the personal data held about you or your child, have it corrected or updated, ask for it to be erased where the school no longer needs it, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. Because the school controls the records, the fastest route is your school office; you may also contact us (§10) and we will pass the request to the school and help fulfil it. You may complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you believe your rights have been infringed.

9. Deleting your account

Accounts are created by the school, not by you, so deletion is arranged with the school. You can request it in the app under Account → Delete my account, or from our account deletion page. The page explains exactly what is deleted and what the school must keep by law.
