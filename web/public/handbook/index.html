01

Overview

One place a whole school runs on, and the same place a parent checks a fee from a phone.

The system is organised around roles. When you sign in you land in the workspace for your job: an accounts clerk sees fee counters and receipts, a class teacher sees registers and marks, a parent sees only their own child. No school ever sees another's data, and inside a school people see only what their role allows.

You never go looking for a feature in the abstract, you open the workspace for the job in front of you, and the feature is there.

What it covers
  • Admissions & enrolment, enquiries, application forms, document checks, fee & seat allocation, promotion and year rollover.
  • Fees & finance, fee structures, counter collection, receipts, concessions, arrears, reminders, cheque/UPI reconciliation, GST.
  • Attendance, student registers and staff attendance, biometric devices, absence alerts to guardians.
  • Academics & exams, classes, sections, subjects, timetable, marks, grading and printable report cards.
  • Staff & payroll, the staff directory, leave rules, payroll, statutory PF/ESI/PT/TDS.
  • Operations, transport & live bus tracking, library, hostel, stores, mid-day meal.
  • Communication, circulars, messages, SMS & WhatsApp, and the parent/student portal.
Two ways to look at it. Every account switches freely between the Sidebar layout (a classic left menu) and the Focus layout (a clean board with a floating dock). Same features, different frame, under Settings → Appearance → Layout.
02

Getting started

Five minutes from signing in to knowing your way around.

  1. Sign in.Use the login your school gave you; you land in your role's home. Hold more than one role? Switch between them in Settings → Role switch, and the whole menu changes to match.
  2. Find your way.In Sidebar, the left menu lists your workspaces and screens. In Focus, the floating dock has Home, All features and search. Either way, Ctrl K opens search anywhere, type a screen's name and jump.
  3. Open Settings.The gear (bottom of the dock, or the top bar). Appearance holds layout, language, typeface, text size, density and theme, all remembered on your device.
  4. Set your language.Settings → Appearance → Language → English or తెలుగు. It changes only your view, immediately.
  5. Ask the assistant.The glowing orb answers "how do I…" and opens the right screen for you. Chapter 4.
03

Everyday workflows

The jobs most people open the system to do, each one, step by step.

01

Admit a student

From a first enquiry to a child sitting in a section.

  1. Open Admissions → Enquiries and add the enquiry (name, class sought, a phone). Assign a counsellor so someone owns the follow-up.
  2. When the family applies, take the form under Applications and upload the documents (birth certificate, Aadhaar, last report card).
  3. Mark each document verified, or rejected, with the reason the parent is told.
  4. Under Fee & Enrollment, allocate the fee plan and the seat, then confirm, the child becomes a student in a class and section, and a parent login is offered.
Admissions → Enquiries → Applications → Fee & Enrollment
02

Collect a fee

Cash, card or UPI at the counter, with a receipt.

  1. Open Finance → Fee counter and search the student by name or admission number.
  2. Their outstanding invoices are listed; tick the ones being paid. Concessions and any carried-forward arrears are already applied.
  3. Choose the mode (cash / card / UPI / cheque), enter the amount, and record the payment.
  4. The receipt is generated with a gapless number; print or share it. It also lands in the parent's portal.
Finance → Fee counter · Receipts
03

Mark attendance

A class's register for the day.

  1. Open Faculty → Attendance → Take attendance and pick your section and the date.
  2. The roll loads; mark each child present, absent, late, half-day or on leave. Default is present, so you only touch the exceptions.
  3. Save. Guardians of absentees can be alerted by SMS/portal; biometric devices feed in automatically where fitted.
  4. Got one wrong? Attendance correction fixes a mark and keeps an audit trail of the change.
Faculty → Attendance → Take attendance
04

Enter marks & print report cards

From an exam's marks to a card the head signs.

  1. Under Exams, open the exam and set each subject's max marks (a teacher may do this).
  2. In the Gradebook, enter each student's marks per subject; the percentage and grade compute against the school's grade scale.
  3. Generate report cards on the school's own template, the marks, a performance chart and attendance fill in automatically.
  4. Send them up; the head approves, and the approved card carries both signatures and can be printed or released to parents.
Exams → Gradebook · Report cards
05

Send a circular

One notice, to exactly the people it concerns.

  1. Open Communication → Circulars and write it once, a holiday, a datesheet, an emergency.
  2. Choose the audience: a class, a section, all parents, staff, the circular goes only to them.
  3. Pick the channels: the portal, SMS and email. Send.
  4. Track who has read it; resend to just those who have not.
Communication → Circulars
06

Run payroll

The month, with statutory deductions and payslips.

  1. The staff directory already carries each person's pay structure and bank details.
  2. Under HR → Payroll, start the month's run; salaries, allowances and PF, ESI, PT and TDS are computed.
  3. Review the run, then finalise it.
  4. Payslips are issued to staff, and the statutory registers are ready under Statutory.
HR → Payroll · Statutory
07

Track the bus

Who boards where, and where the bus is now.

  1. Under Operations → Transport, assign each child a route and a stop; the fee slab follows from the distance.
  2. Live tracking shows every bus on a map in real time.
  3. Parents follow their child's bus from the portal and get a proximity alert as it nears their stop.
Operations → Transport · Live tracking
08

Promote a class at year end

A whole section up a year, without losing anyone's history.

  1. Open Lifecycle → Promotion and choose the section moving up and the class it moves into.
  2. The whole roll is selected by default; untick anyone held back.
  3. Confirm, each child's old enrolment is closed and a new one opened, so the history survives, and unpaid arrears carry forward.
  4. Year rollover does the same across the whole school when the new session opens.
Lifecycle → Promotion · Year rollover
04

The assistant

A help orb in the corner that answers in your own words.

Press the glowing orb and ask a plain question, where do I collect a fee, how do I change the language to Telugu, which class is this student in. It answers in a few sentences, grounded in the screens you can open, and where an answer names a screen it hands you a button to open it. It reads the answer aloud in a natural voice, and takes your question by microphone.

The assistant explains and points; it never changes anything on its own, and only ever sees data your role already allows. Turn the voice on with the speaker button inside the chat.
05

Settings & language

Everything you can personalise, and where it lives.

  • Language · Appearance → Language (English / తెలుగు). Per device, immediate.
  • Layout · Sidebar or Focus, under Appearance.
  • Theme, light, dark, or follow-the-device; it cross-fades.
  • Type & density, typeface, text size, density and corner rounding.
  • Colours, the Colour tab, for the accent your boards draw with.
  • Dock & dashboard, arrange the Focus dock and your home cards.
  • Role switch, move between the workspaces your account holds.
  • Security, change your password and set up two-factor sign-in.
