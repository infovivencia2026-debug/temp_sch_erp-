# Target audience and content — answers for WISEN

Play Console → Policy → App content → **Target audience and content**.

## Age groups the app is DESIGNED FOR
Tick **only**: **18 and over**.

Do **not** tick any under-18 group. Reasoning to keep in mind (and to give in the
free-text box if asked): the app's users are **parents/guardians and school staff**.
Accounts are created by the school and issued to adults; there is no self sign-up
and no feature aimed at children. The app *stores information about* students
(most of them minors) as records the school keeps in the course of education —
which is fundamentally different from an app *directed at* children.

Consequences of this answer:
- The app is **not** in the Designed for Families programme and must not be marketed
  as a kids' app.
- Play may still ask: "Could your app unintentionally appeal to children?" → **No**
  (dashboards of fees, attendance registers and receipts; no games, cartoons or
  child-oriented design).
- Because the app contains children's personal data, keep the privacy policy's
  children section (§4) accurate: data is recorded by the school under parental
  consent obtained at admission; no profiling or advertising.
