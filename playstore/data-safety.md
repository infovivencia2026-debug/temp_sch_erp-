Play Console → Policy → App content → **Data safety**. Answer exactly as below;
every answer is derived from what the app and its backend actually do (see the
"Evidence" column). If behaviour changes, change this file and the form together.

## Data types — what to tick

"Collected" = sent off the device to our servers. "Shared" = passed to a third party.
Ephemeral = not stored beyond the request. All collection below is **required** for the
app to work (not optional), and **none** of it is used for advertising or analytics.

| Category | Data type | Collected | Shared | Purpose(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal info** | Name | Yes | No | App functionality, Account management | Name on the account, set by the school |
| Personal info | Email address | Yes | No | App functionality, Account management | Sign-in identifier (may be phone instead) |
| Personal info | Phone number | Yes | No | App functionality, Account management | Sign-in identifier / guardian contact |
| Personal info | User IDs | Yes | No | App functionality, Account management | Account id, admission number |
| Personal info | Address | Yes | No | App functionality | Guardian address held by the school (shown on profile) |
| Personal info | Other info | Yes | No | App functionality | Student records: attendance, marks, timetable, transport stop, health notes the school records |
| **Financial info** | Purchase history | Yes | No | App functionality | Fee invoices, receipts, payment history, wallet transactions. **No** card numbers or bank credentials are ever collected — payments happen in the user's own UPI/bank app. Do **not** tick "Credit card, debit card or bank account number". |
| **Messages** | Other in-app messages | Yes | No | App functionality | School ↔ family messages, forum posts, absence follow-up notes |
| **Photos and videos** | Photos | Yes (optional, user-initiated) | No | App functionality | Profile photo / documents the user chooses to upload |
| **Files and docs** | Files and docs | Yes (optional, user-initiated) | No | App functionality | Documents uploaded by the user; receipts downloaded to the device are not collected |
| **App activity** | — | **No** | — | — | No analytics SDK, no in-app search logging sent for analysis |

| **Device or other IDs** | Device or other IDs | Yes | **Yes → Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging)** | App functionality (push notifications) | The FCM registration token is generated by Firebase and sent to our server; Google processes it to deliver notifications. This is the one "shared" row. |
| **Location** | Approximate / Precise | **No** | — | — | The parent app has no location permission and no geolocation handler. Bus positions are collected by the separate driver app, not by this one. |
| Contacts, Calendar, Audio, Health & fitness, Web browsing, Installed apps | — | **No** | — | — | Not accessed |

For every "Collected = Yes" row also answer:
- **Is this data processed ephemerally?** No (it is stored on the school's server).
- **Is this data required or optional?** Required — except Photos and Files, which are **Optional** (the user chooses to upload).

## Why these answers are safe to give
- Server: Go API under FORCE row-level security per school; passwords hashed (`PASSWORD_PEPPER` + salted hash); no ad or analytics SDK anywhere in the web or Android code.
- Android: dependencies are Firebase Messaging only (`mobile/apps/parent/gradle/libs.versions.toml`); `allowBackup="false"`; offline cache is cleared on sign-out (`web/src/sw-src.js`, `/logout` deletes both caches).
- The only third party that receives per-user data is Google (FCM token). Cloudflare, Google Cloud and Neon are infrastructure processors, not "sharing" in Play's definition (service providers acting on our instructions).
