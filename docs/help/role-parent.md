# Using the app as Parent / Guardian

Your child at this school. Fees, attendance, results, the bus, and anything the school has sent you.

You have 10 screens. They are grouped the way the sidebar groups them: a workspace holds sections, and a section holds the screens themselves. Your workspace key is `parent`, which is the first part of every address in this role.

Each entry below is the name you will see in the sidebar, followed by what that screen is for and the data it can reach.

## My Child

### Academics

**Homework & academics.** Homework, classwork, subjects and published academic progress.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → My Child → Academics → Homework & academics
- What it can see: Linked child/children only
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, seller_admin, student, super_admin

**Results & report cards.** Exam schedule, marks/grades and downloadable published report cards.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → My Child → Academics → Results & report cards
- What it can see: Linked child/children only
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, seller_admin, student, super_admin

### Attendance

**Attendance.** Daily/monthly attendance and absence history for the selected child.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → My Child → Attendance → Attendance
- What it can see: Linked child/children only
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, seller_admin, student, super_admin

## Requests

### Consent

**Consent & acknowledgement.** Acknowledge circulars, permissions, trips and configured consent forms.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Requests → Consent → Consent & acknowledgement
- What it can see: Linked child/children only
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, seller_admin, student, super_admin

**Digital Parent Consent Slips.** Provide digital signature consent for upcoming field trips, sports events, or health camps.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Requests → Consent → Digital Parent Consent Slips
- What it can see: Specific student
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, seller_admin, student, super_admin

## Fees

### Fees

**Fees & payments.** Dues, installments, online payment, payment history and receipts.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Fees → Fees & payments
- What it can see: Linked child/children only
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, seller_admin, student, super_admin

## Home

### Home

**Child summary.** Attendance today/%, homework due, latest result, fee due and latest announcement for selected child.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Home → Child summary
- What it can see: Linked child/children only
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, seller_admin, student, super_admin

**Child switcher.** Switch between linked children without separate accounts.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Home → Child switcher
- What it can see: Linked child/children only
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, seller_admin, student, super_admin

**Needs attention.** Absence, attendance shortage, overdue fee, consent request, unread circular or upcoming PTM.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Home → Needs attention
- What it can see: Linked child/children only
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, seller_admin, student, super_admin

## School

### Messages

**Communication.** Circulars, announcements and permitted teacher/school messages.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → School → Messages → Communication
- What it can see: Linked child/children only
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, seller_admin, student, super_admin


If a screen listed here is not in your sidebar, it is because nothing has been assigned to you for it yet -- a teacher with no section, a head of department with no department. The permission is real and the workspace is empty; ask the office to make the assignment rather than waiting for a release.
