# Using the app as Faculty / Teacher

Your classes, your subjects, and the children in them. Attendance in the morning, marks at the end of a term, and a record of anything that needs the office to know.

You have 32 screens. They are grouped the way the sidebar groups them: a workspace holds sections, and a section holds the screens themselves. Your workspace key is `faculty`, which is the first part of every address in this role.

Each entry below is the name you will see in the sidebar, followed by what that screen is for and the data it can reach.

## My Classes

### Attendance

**Attendance correction.** Request or process correction within permitted window/workflow.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → My Classes → Attendance → Attendance correction
- What it can see: Self + assigned classes/subjects
- Also visible to: hod, institution_admin, super_admin

**Take attendance.** Fast daily or period/subject-wise attendance for assigned students.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → My Classes → Attendance → Take attendance
- What it can see: Self + assigned classes/subjects
- Also visible to: admissions, finance, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, super_admin

If a screen listed here is not in your sidebar, it is because nothing has been assigned to you for it yet -- a teacher with no section, a head of department with no department. The permission is real and the workspace is empty; ask the office to make the assignment rather than waiting for a release.
