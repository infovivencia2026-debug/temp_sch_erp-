# Using the app as Seller Admin

The schools using this product: what each one has, what it costs to provide, and what they have asked for.

You have 7 screens. They are grouped the way the sidebar groups them: a workspace holds sections, and a section holds the screens themselves. Your workspace key is `seller_admin`, which is the first part of every address in this role.

Each entry below is the name you will see in the sidebar, followed by what that screen is for and the data it can reach.

## Customers

### Customers

**Onboarding Progress.** How far each new school has got through setup, so the account manager can intervene before a stalled rollout becomes a cancellation.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Customers → Onboarding Progress
- What it can see: All institutions / all campuses
- Also visible to: super_admin

**Provision New School.** Create a tenant, its first campus and its first administrator in one step, and send the welcome credentials.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Customers → Provision New School
- What it can see: All institutions / all campuses
- Also visible to: super_admin

**Suspend & Reactivate.** Suspend a tenant for non-payment or at end of contract, blocking sign-in while preserving the data, and reactivate on settlement.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Customers → Suspend & Reactivate
- What it can see: All institutions / all campuses
- Also visible to: super_admin

**Tenant Directory.** Every school on the installation with its plan, student headcount, status and date joined; open one to act inside it for support.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Customers → Tenant Directory
- What it can see: All institutions / all campuses
- Also visible to: super_admin

## Subscriptions & Billing

### Subscriptions & Billing

**Plans & Pricing.** Define plans with a student cap, included modules and an annual or per-student price.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Subscriptions & Billing → Plans & Pricing
- What it can see: All institutions / all campuses
- Also visible to: super_admin

**Seat Overage & Renewals.** Schools past their student cap or inside the renewal window, with the revenue at risk.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Subscriptions & Billing → Seat Overage & Renewals
- What it can see: All institutions / all campuses
- Also visible to: super_admin

**Subscription Ledger.** Each school's plan, term dates, renewal date and invoice history.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Subscriptions & Billing → Subscription Ledger
- What it can see: All institutions / all campuses
- Also visible to: super_admin


If a screen listed here is not in your sidebar, it is because nothing has been assigned to you for it yet -- a teacher with no section, a head of department with no department. The permission is real and the workspace is empty; ask the office to make the assignment rather than waiting for a release.
