# How to do the usual things

Each recipe names the role that does it and the exact path through the sidebar.
If a screen named here is missing from your sidebar, see "When a screen says it
is empty" in *Finding your way around* — it is nearly always an assignment that
has not been made, not a fault.

## Money

**Collect a fee at the counter.** Accounts & Finance → Fees → Collections →
Collect payment. Search the student to open their fee account and take the
payment; the receipt is raised against that account and is reprintable from
Collections → Receipts. Part payments and payments in advance are handled by
Partial & advance payments rather than by adjusting the amount here.

**Collect an admission fee.** Admissions & Front Office → Admissions →
Admissions → Admission Fee Collection. This is separate from the counter
because at that point the child is an applicant rather than a student on roll.

**See who owes money.** Accounts & Finance → Fees → Student Dues → Defaulters
& reminders, which lists students in arrears with the amount outstanding and
lets you send the reminder. For one family's full history rather than the list,
use Student Dues → Student ledger.

**Reconcile the day's takings.** Accounts & Finance → Fees → Reconciliation.
Payments taken online are matched separately under Payment Gateway
Reconciliation, because the gateway settles on its own timetable.

**Set what a class is charged.** Accounts & Finance → Fees → Fee Structure →
Class-Wise Fee Structure Configuration, after the heads themselves exist under
Fee Head & Group Setup. Heads first, then structure: a structure can only
combine heads that have been defined.

**Give a family a concession.** Accounts & Finance → Fees → Concessions &
Refunds → Discounts / scholarships. Above a threshold it is routed to the Principal for
sign-off rather than applied directly, and it appears in their Approvals queue.

## Children and their day

**Take attendance.** Faculty / Teacher → My Classes → Attendance → Take
attendance. It opens on your own section for today. If you marked the wrong
child, use Attendance correction in the same place rather than re-marking the
register — a correction is recorded as a correction.

**Enter marks.** Faculty / Teacher → Assessments → Marks & Report Cards → Marks
entry, for the exam and subject you are entering. The Principal publishes
results from Academics → Examinations once entry is complete; entering marks
does not by itself show them to a parent.

**Note something about a child.** Faculty / Teacher → Communication → Remarks,
or Class Teacher Remarks if you are the class teacher. This is the record the
office reads, so write it as something a parent may eventually be shown.

## Admissions

**Take an enquiry through to a place.** Admissions & Front Office → Admissions.
The order is enquiry, application, decision (Offers / admission decisions),
admission fee, then the documents — Aadhaar & APAAR Capture at Admission, and
Transfer Certificate Intake where the child is coming from another school. A
child who cannot be given a place yet goes on Admission Waitlist Management
rather than being refused.

## Staff

**Apply for leave.** Faculty / Teacher → My Work → My Profile → Leave & self
service. It goes to HR, and to your head of department where one is set.

**Run payroll.** HR & Payroll → Payroll. Attendance and leave for the month
have to be settled first, because deductions are computed from them — see
Attendance & Leave. The bank file is generated from Direct Bank Payroll
Transfer File once the run is approved.

## Approvals

**Clear what is waiting on you.** Institution Admin / Principal → Home →
Approvals. Everything raised for your decision is in one queue, oldest at the
top, filtered by kind with the chips along the header. Deciding here is what
releases the thing the other person is waiting on, so it is worth clearing
daily rather than weekly.

## For families

**See what my child owes, and pay it.** Parent / Guardian → Fees → Fees &
payments.

**See attendance and results.** Parent / Guardian → My Child → Attendance, and
My Child → Academics → Results & report cards. Results appear only once the
school has published them; a blank result is usually "not published yet" rather
than "not entered".

**Read what the school has sent.** Parent / Guardian → School → Messages →
Communication, or the bell in the top bar for the same thing as it arrives.

**Answer a consent slip.** Parent / Guardian → Requests → Consent. A trip or a
medical form the school needs a yes or no on; answering here is the record,
so nothing has to come back on paper.

**Switch between your children.** Parent / Guardian → Home → Child switcher.
Every other screen in this role then follows the child you have chosen.
