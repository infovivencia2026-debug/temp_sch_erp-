# Using the app as HR & Payroll

Everybody the school employs: joining, leave, salary, and the statutory filings that follow from all three.

You have 37 screens. They are grouped the way the sidebar groups them: a workspace holds sections, and a section holds the screens themselves. Your workspace key is `hr`, which is the first part of every address in this role.

Each entry below is the name you will see in the sidebar, followed by what that screen is for and the data it can reach.

## Attendance & Leave

### Attendance

**Staff attendance.** Employee attendance, late/early records and attendance reports.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Attendance & Leave → Attendance → Staff attendance
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus employees
- Also visible to: institution_admin, super_admin

### Leave

**Half-Day Leave Deduction Calculation.** Auto-calculate half-day salary deductions when staff check out earlier than prescribed shift hours.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Attendance & Leave → Leave → Half-Day Leave Deduction Calculation
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus
- Also visible to: hod, institution_admin, super_admin

**Late Arrival & Loss of Pay (LOP).** Configure auto-deduction rules for late arrivals or unexcused absences leading to LOP.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Attendance & Leave → Leave → Late Arrival & Loss of Pay (LOP)
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus
- Also visible to: hod, institution_admin, super_admin

**Leave.** Leave balances, requests, approvals and holiday calendar.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Attendance & Leave → Leave → Leave
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus employees
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, finance, hod, institution_admin, operations, parent, seller_admin, student, super_admin

**Leave Policy Configuration.** Define Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Earned Leave, Maternity Leave rules and carry-forward limits.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Attendance & Leave → Leave → Leave Policy Configuration
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus
- Also visible to: hod, institution_admin, super_admin
