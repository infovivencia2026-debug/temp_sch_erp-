# Using the app as Accounts & Finance

You collect fees, chase what is owed, and answer for the numbers at the end of the month. Most days start at Fee Counter and end at the day book.

You have 19 screens. They are grouped the way the sidebar groups them: a workspace holds sections, and a section holds the screens themselves. Your workspace key is `finance`, which is the first part of every address in this role.

Each entry below is the name you will see in the sidebar, followed by what that screen is for and the data it can reach.

## Fees

### Collections

**Collect payment.** Record cash/bank/online/manual payments with controls and receipt generation.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Fees → Collections → Collect payment
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus finance scope
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, hod, institution_admin, operations, super_admin

**Online payments.** Track gateway payments and payment status.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Fees → Collections → Online payments
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus finance scope
- Also visible to: institution_admin, super_admin

**Partial & advance payments.** Handle permitted partial collection, advances and credit application.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Fees → Collections → Partial & advance payments
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus finance scope
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, hod, institution_admin, operations, super_admin

**Receipts.** Generate, reprint and verify fee receipts.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Fees → Collections → Receipts
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus finance scope
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, hod, institution_admin, operations, super_admin

### Concessions & Refunds

- Also visible to: institution_admin, super_admin

**Multi-Level Concession Approvals.** Route high-value fee concession requests to Principal/Management for digital sign-off.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Fees → Concessions & Refunds → Multi-Level Concession Approvals
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus

**Refunds.** Create and process approved refunds.

- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus finance scope
- Also visible to: institution_admin, super_admin

### Fee Structure

**Class-Wise Fee Structure Configuration.** Map fee components, installment amounts, and due dates per class, section, or quota.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Fees → Fee Structure → Class-Wise Fee Structure Configuration
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, parent, seller_admin, student, super_admin

**Demand / invoice generation.** Generate student fee demands/invoices for applicable periods.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Fees → Fee Structure → Demand / invoice generation
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus finance scope
- Also visible to: institution_admin, super_admin

**Fee Head & Group Setup.** Define fee heads (Tuition, Transport, Admission, Lab, Sports) and frequency schedules.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Fees → Fee Structure → Fee Head & Group Setup
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, parent, seller_admin, student, super_admin

### Student Dues

**Cheque Bounce Fine Engine.** Automatically levy penalty charges to student ledger upon cheque dishonor notice.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Fees → Student Dues → Cheque Bounce Fine Engine

- Also visible to: institution_admin, super_admin

- Also visible to: institution_admin, super_admin

**Student ledger.** Single account view of charges, payments, credits, refunds and outstanding balance.

- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus finance scope

## Reports

### Reports

**Finance reports.** Daily collection, outstanding, aging/defaulters, student ledger, payment mode, discount and refund reports.

- Where to find it: Sidebar → Reports → Finance reports
- What it can see: Assigned institution/campus finance scope
- Also visible to: admissions, faculty, hod, hr, institution_admin, operations, parent, seller_admin, student, super_admin


If a screen listed here is not in your sidebar, it is because nothing has been assigned to you for it yet -- a teacher with no section, a head of department with no department. The permission is real and the workspace is empty; ask the office to make the assignment rather than waiting for a release.
