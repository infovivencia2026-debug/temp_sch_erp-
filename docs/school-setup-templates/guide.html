Setting up a school

The seven sheets, and what goes in them

Written with the column names a school's own export already uses, so the sheet you have is the sheet you upload. Every one was run through the live importer before it was put here.

Checked before it is written

Every upload is a dry run first. It names the wrong rows by number, with the values. A file with one bad row writes nothing at all.

Your column names are fine

Do not rename anything. Upload the sheet you already have and the screen asks which of your columns is which. A column we have no field for is kept under your own label.

An upload can be undone

Each upload is listed with who did it and when, and removes only what it created — never a record somebody typed by hand.

Order matters in three places

  • Staff before subjects, if you want teacher names in that sheet.
  • Classes before subjects and before fee structures.
  • Fee heads before fee structures.
  • Students before past years, past results and staff service.

Everything else can go in any order. A sheet that names something not yet created is rejected row by row and tells you what is missing.

01

Classes and sections

01-classes-and-sections.csv

One row per class, with its sections written beside it. This used to be two sheets and the second only worked after the first had landed — so doing them the wrong way round rejected everything.

Class,Sections,Capacity
Pre Nursery,(none),30
Nursery,A,30
Class 1,"A, B",40
Class
The class as the school says it. The year is read from it, so Class 10 sorts below Class 9 with nothing to fill in. Nursery, LKG and UKG are understood.
Sections
As you say them — A, B or Rose, Newton. Leave empty for a class whose sections are not decided yet. (none) is read as empty, because that is what an export writes for a class that has none.
Capacity
Seats per section. Defaults to 40. A section with no capacity can never be full, so admissions can never warn you.

03

Subjects, classes and teachers

03-subjects-classes-teachers.csv

One sheet doing three jobs. What a row does depends on what it carries — so a plain list of subject names is a valid file, a full allocation grid is a valid file, and so is anything between.

subject,class,section,room,class_teacher_email,teacher_email,max_marks,periods_per_week
Sanskrit,,,,,,,
English,Class 1,,,,,100,6
Mathematics,Class 6,A,6A,priya@school.in,anand@school.in,100,6
subject alone
Adds the subject. A subject the school has not created yet is created from this sheet — there is no separate subject list to do first.
+ class
That class studies it.
+ section
That section only, and its room. Leave it empty and a named teacher covers every section of the class, which is what a small school means.
+ teacher_email
Who teaches the subject. class_teacher_email is who takes that section as a form class.
periods_per_week
What the timetable solver reads. Without it, generating a draft has nothing to work from.

05

The school day

05-school-day.csv

Periods and breaks, in order. Breaks are listed too — the timetable needs them to know a teacher is free, and attendance needs them to know a period was not taught.

Sequence,Period,Starts,Ends,Break
1,Period 1,09:00,09:45,No
3,Short break,10:30,10:45,Yes

A school whose primary section runs different hours names a second day on the screen — Primary, Afternoon shift — and ticks which classes use it. Grade 1 then changes lesson when Grade 1 changes lesson, not when Grade 10 does.

06

Fee heads

06-fee-heads.csv

What the school charges for, before deciding what any class pays.

Fee Head,Code,Recurring
Tuition,TUI,Yes
Admission,ADM,No
Code
What a receipt prints, and what a second upload matches on — so a corrected sheet edits the head rather than adding a twin.
Recurring
Yes for anything billed every term. No for a one-off like an admission fee.
07

Fee structures

07-fee-structures.csv

One row per head per class. Needs sheets 01 and 06 first: the class and the head are both matched by name.

Structure,Class,Fee Head,Annual Amount,Instalments
Nursery 2026-27,Nursery,Tuition,36000,3
Nursery 2026-27,Nursery,Books and stationery,4500,1
Structure
The name groups the rows. Every row carrying the same name is one price list, so a class's whole fee is written down the sheet rather than across it.
Annual Amount
The year's figure for that head. Rupees as you write them.
Instalments
How many times a year it is billed — 3 for a termly fee, 1 for something charged once. The amount is divided across them.
08

Past years, per child

08-past-years-per-child.csv only if the school ran before

One row per child per year. Attendance as a total, fees as a figure — which is what a school actually hands over, rather than four years of registers and receipts.

admission_no,year,class,days_present,days_total,fee_billed,fee_paid,fee_waived,notes
ADM0001,2025-26,Class 5,187,210,35500,32500,3000,Promoted with distinction
days_present
days_total
Out of the days the school actually ran. Present above total is refused — it prints as attendance over 100%.
fee_billed
fee_paid
Rupees as you write them; 28,000 is fine. Held apart from this year's collection, so a closed year is never counted as today's takings.
09

Past results

09-past-results-grid.csv only if the school ran before

The mark sheet as a staff room keeps it — children down, subjects across, one mark in a cell. Not one row per subject: forty children across six subjects is 240 rows, and nobody keeps marks that way.

Adm No,Name,English,Maths,Hindi,Science,Total,Rank
ADM0001,Ravi Teja,72,81,64,77,294,3
ADM0002,Sana Fatima,68,AB,71,,139,9
the columns
Name the subject each marks column holds when you upload. Leave Total and Rank unmapped — they are worked out from the marks, not read from the sheet.
AB
Absent, which is a result. A blank cell is a subject that child does not take — not a zero.
asked once
The year, the exam, the class and what the papers are out of are asked above the file, not repeated on every row.

Staff service

10-staff-service.csv only if the school ran before

Years served before this system. Without it, an imported teacher of eleven years' standing has worked here since the morning of the upload.

employee_code,year,designation,days_present,days_total,leaves_taken,notes
T-001,2025-26,Senior Teacher,212,220,8,Class teacher 6-A

This is what a school reaches for when it writes an experience certificate, settles seniority, or answers an inspector asking how long somebody has taught a subject.

Grading scale
A1 91–100, A2 81–90, and so on. Marks cannot become grades without it.
An exam
Name, kind, dates, grading scale.

Eleven digits. Needed before the annual return can be filed.
